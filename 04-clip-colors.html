Stage 04

Clip colors

Every wedding

Colors are how the edit finds its material. Paint every clip you keep — the color says what it is, so nobody has to scrub the timeline to find the first look.

How to paint a clip

Select the clip (or several), then Mark ▸ Set Clip Color — or right-click the clip and pick Clip Color. Resolve shows these names in English; the grey name under each one is the Spanish Juan sees on his machine.

The palette

Nine colors, in the order the day happens. These are the only ones we use — anything else in that menu is not part of the system.

Beigebeige Day or night before the wedding. rehearsal dinner, family arriving, welcome party
Pinkrosado The bride — anything that is about her. bridal party, mom dressing, getting ready, portraits of her alone
Purplemorado The groom — his side of the morning. groomsmen, getting ready, portraits of him alone
Orangenaranja Ceremony, end to end. from guests walking in to the couple walking out
Violetvioleta The couple together. first look, kissing, photo session, cutting the cake not the dances, not the party — those are Navy
Tancanela Guests. messages to camera, their photo sessions, cocktail hour
Oliveverde oliva Reception. couple's entrance, dinner, speeches
Navyazul marino Dancing — first dances and the party. first dance, parent dances, everything on the dance floor
Limelima Scenery — the place, with nobody in it. venue, décor, tables, floral arrangements the moment a person is the subject, it belongs to their color
No colorsin color Doesn't fit any of them? Leave it grey. an unpainted clip is a question for Juan, not a mistake

Two rules

Paint only what you keep. The color is the selection — a clip you wouldn't put in the edit stays grey.

One color per clip. If a take spans two moments, pick the one it belongs to in the story, not the one it starts in.
